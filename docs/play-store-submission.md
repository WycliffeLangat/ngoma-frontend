## Store Listing Draft

Short description:

Kenya's official multi-platform music charts, artist rankings, records, and music analytics.

Full description:

Ngoma Charts tracks the music performing strongly across major digital platforms and turns that activity into clear monthly charts, artist rankings, analytics, records, and certifications.

Follow Top 50 singles and albums, compare artists and releases, explore platform performance, read chart news, and discover the records shaping Kenya's music landscape.

Features:
- Combined Top 50 singles and albums
- Platform charts across major music services
- Artist rankings and release profiles
- Chart analytics, records, and head-to-head comparisons
- Certifications and music news

Ngoma Charts is a product of Ngoma Media Ltd.
