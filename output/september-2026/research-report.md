# September 2026 new-entry metadata

Reviewed 91 distinct releases appearing as new entries in the September 2026 charts. Applied 540 source-backed fields to 75 releases and 28 artist profiles. Existing nonempty values were preserved.

Country details also propagated to 22 related releases with empty country fields, using their verified primary artist. Chart snapshots and derived history were synchronized.

Album songwriter lists combine the credited writers available on the source track listings. A copyright holder is retained as the label attribution shown by the catalogue; no distributor is inferred from a label name.
